Tattoo Aftercare

✓

CARE:

  1. Keep bandage on for at least 4 hours, less than 12 hours.
  2. Take off bandage and wash thoroughly with water & mild soap.
  3. Pat dry & keep moist. Put on very, very thin layers of AQUAPHOR / Skin Cream / Moisturizer 3 times a day for 2 weeks.
  4. Consult with a physician if you have signs of infection.

DO NOT:

  1. DO NOT pick or scratch your new tattoo. If it itches, slap it lightly.
  2. DO NOT expose your tattoo to the sun.
  3. DO NOT soak your tattoo in bath, jacuzzi, swimming pool, ocean or river for 2 weeks. Shower is fine.
  4. DO NOT rebandage tattoo.
  5. DO NOT shave tattoo for 2 weeks.
  6. DO NOT wear tight clothes that will rub off scab.
  7. DO NOT let pets rub on your new tattoo.
